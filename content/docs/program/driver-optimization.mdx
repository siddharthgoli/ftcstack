# Driver Optimization

Driver optimization is the work of making the robot easier and faster to operate in real matches. This includes input shaping, feedback, and control layouts that reduce mistakes.

## FTC connection

The best TeleOp is the one the driver trusts during a match.

## Common mistakes

- Prioritizing cleverness over reliability.
- Changing controls without training the drivers.
- Ignoring what the driver actually experiences on the field.
