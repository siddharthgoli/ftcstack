# Feedforward

Feedforward estimates the power a mechanism needs before feedback corrects the remaining error. It is useful when gravity, motion, or friction are predictable enough to model.

## FTC connection

It is common in arms, slides, and drivetrain motion where a direct correction loop alone is not enough.

## Common mistakes

- Treating feedforward as a replacement for feedback.
- Ignoring the real mechanism’s friction and load.
- Copying constants between unrelated mechanisms.
