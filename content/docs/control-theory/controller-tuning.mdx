# Controller Tuning

Controller tuning is the practical work of changing gains and observing behavior until the robot acts the way you want. It is a measurement process, not a guessing game.

## FTC connection

Tune on the real robot, with the real mechanism, under real load.

## Common mistakes

- Changing too many variables at once.
- Tuning without telemetry.
- Copying values from another robot and expecting the same result.
